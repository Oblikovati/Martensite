# Closed-extrusion face validation

This operation consumes an immutable coherent face input. It admits a full closed quadratic spline extrusion strip with line pcurves, matching cap spline bases and a genuine paired seam. Exact corner and located vertex incidences establish the rectangle quotient. Stored UV endpoint caches must match those exact corners. The existing whole-support certificate proves injectivity on that quotient; exact metric decisions separately bind stored curves and endpoints to the support within their unchanged raw limits.

A coefficient upper bound can establish conformance of a full cap through the shared positive normalized basis. Conformance within a raw tolerance does not prove that an altered spatial boundary is injective. The default `ClosedFacePolicy::bounded()` selects the coincident-boundary contract. It requires exact identity of all represented spatial boundary and endpoint relations to the trimmed support before transferring its self-exclusion proof. A nonzero or inconclusive relation leaves self-exclusion incomplete while retaining the metric conformance evidence and unchanged limits. Failure of that sufficient bound leaves the check incomplete. An actual endpoint or stationary residual witness can establish a consistency violation. Unsupported representations, unresolved support separation, cancellation and exhausted work cannot publish a certificate.

`ClosedFacePolicy::piecewise_boundary()` explicitly selects a second contract. The ideal support and an independently certified actual carrier each retain their own proof. Exact coefficient relations bind both actual caps, both seam branches and every vertex to that carrier. A common coordinate functional separates cap levels from the open ideal surface interior; the seam must also coincide exactly with the ideal seam. Both proofs consume one shared span, obligation and proof-work allowance. Source coefficients and the original parameter interval remain unchanged.

The piecewise contract proves separate injectivity of the ideal surface and the actual boundary, with the stated exact joins and stored metric conformance. It does not prove that different spatial and parametric representations of the same boundary edge form an exactly embedded continuous union. Callers requiring coincident representations must retain the default contract. Folded or collapsed actual carriers, unresolved transverse separation and inexact required joins remain incomplete regardless of a large metric tolerance.

Locations are replayed on owned datums with preserved aliases and powers. Every replay must agree bit-for-bit with its actual captured cache before it is used. Shortcut transform forms must also agree with their stored affine coefficients. Source coefficients are kept separate from scales and affine placements; no rounded transformed poles or sampled geometry are acceptance evidence.

The report owns its input, requested policy, ordered coverage, actual work, certified error uppers and read-only history. It describes the represented trimmed support and its declared consistency checks. Separation of tolerance-thickened surfaces, other faces, solids and complete modelling operations require their own coverage. A face result selects its surface map by actual owned support and located identity; it does not certify other captured surface maps. A certificate never validates a later-mutated live entity.

Independent polynomial strips, topology and budget negatives, source isolation and process/thread comparisons exercise this Feature #213 operation. Validation results are recorded after the package and pull-request gates run.

Failed exact and metric queries retain their invocation-local source owner, typed relation and original numerical cause. A query-limit outcome also names its specific relation when known. Boundary occurrence addresses in error-bound records remain distinct from source-owner labels; spatial-consistency violations identify the actual source owner (#378).
