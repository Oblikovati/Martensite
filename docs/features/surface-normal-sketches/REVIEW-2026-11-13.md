## Verdict

The proof work is sound: exact binary64 input, outward rounding, typed refusals,
finite and cancellable work. But an early representation choice makes the proof
surface grow without bound, and the work grew bottom-up with no orchestrator.
After six days and about 67k added lines, no operation publishes a body. The
slow fixtures and the refusals are structural, not tuning problems.

Recommendation: stop the seam/collar proof line, change the representation to
rational on-cylinder edges, and build one complete operation first.

## Root causes, ranked

### R1. Representation creates most proof obligations

3D edges are cubic Hermite polynomials (`wrapped_candidate/polynomial.rs:52-70`).
A polynomial curve cannot lie on a cylinder, so every cap edge lies off its cap
surface (stated in WRAPPED-MATH.md, first section). The two depth curves are
fitted independently (`fit.rs:72`), so side rulings are only approximately
radial. Together these create the seam gluing, collar, warp, inverse-cover,
cap/side normal-depth and correction-homotopy proof families. WRAPPED-MATH.md
rejects a "replacement surface family" because it would lose exact cylinder
caps; rational on-cylinder edges keep them and were not considered.

### R2. 2D problems are proved in 3D

On an injective chart with positive radius the cylindrical map is a
diffeomorphism. If the native body is an exact image of a developed profile,
injectivity, separation, joins and holes are developed-plane questions. The
branch instead proves them with 4D box covers over all piece pairs, separating
planes and projections. This dominates the fixture runtime.

### R3. No top-down error budget

Each stage has its own limit. The fit limit (1e-7) equals the cap metric limit
(1e-7), leaving no composition margin. Bounds then fail just above their
limits: 0.1378 vs 0.1 (side column), 2.43e-7 vs 1e-7 (full collar). Stages are
"conditional" and "do not establish …"; no production code discharges those
conditions.

### R4. Proof-engine cost (TKMath)

- Exact BigInt rationals with gcd reduction on every operation, including
  negation (`certificate_arithmetic/rational.rs:26,42,45`).
- Power-basis polynomials, recomposed per cell (`arithmetic.rs` `compose`,
  `ruled/tensor.rs:57`) and converted to Bernstein again with binomials
  recomputed per term (`arithmetic.rs:99-118`): O(n³) checkpoints per hull at
  Taylor degree 24 and above. BOUNDED-CHART.md:209-212 shows projection,
  restriction and hull dominate (39k + 51k + 70k of 165k checkpoints).
- Caches reuse only the root cell (`cap_correspondence/pairs/cache.rs:80-85`,
  `cap_side/seam/root_ranges.rs:38,50`), so the repetition in #436 remains.
- N² pair enumeration without a broad phase (`tksketch wrapped_collection/
  separation.rs:50`, `cap_side/cover.rs:239-243`); fixed-axis separation
  instead of the existing adaptive `bezier_pair/hull_separation.rs`.
- Wasted split axis in `seam/cover.rs:286`.
- No point-witness refutation: a failing query spends its whole budget, which
  is why caps "exhaust 1,000,000 checkpoints".
- Exact Taylor centers grow denominators with depth (`trig.rs:21`,
  `trigonometric.rs:14,33`); peak 2,984 bits.
- Correlation loss: per-axis hulls then sum of squares (`residual.rs:69`);
  triangle-inequality composition in `seam/envelope.rs:98-106`.
- Checkpoints are not weighted by operand size (`budget.rs:12-25`), so caps do
  not predict time (about 4 µs per checkpoint).
- Three copies of the same cover algorithm (`cap_correspondence/pairs.rs:102-252`,
  `cap_side/cover.rs:75-215`, `cap_side/seam/cover.rs:28-303`), plus
  `separation/cover.rs` and `ruled/refinement.rs`. `arithmetic::hull`
  duplicates `certificate_arithmetic::bernstein_enclosure`.

### R5. No orchestrator

Tests call the stages in sequence and act as the orchestrator
(`wrapped_candidate/tests/{cap_side,cap_normal,collection,seams}.rs`,
`tests/native_rebind_all/replay.rs`). `surface_sketch/pipeline.rs:4-44` already
shows the target shape (construct, history, encode, decode, validate, measure,
assemble) and is not reused.

### R6. Shared mutable native topology

Shallow `Shape` clones (`fork.rs:4-53`) and `&mut cap.clone()`
(`native/faces.rs:244`) let stages alias mutable data, so every stage rechecks
operands bit for bit. This is the reason for `rebind`, `rebind_all`, `prepared`,
`replay` and `collection_copy`. Freezing the candidate once into owned bytes
removes this class of code.

### R7. Identity rule violations

- Split names depend on prover exhaustion: `fit.rs:124-146` matches the string
  `resource: "subdivisions"` to split. A policy change renames faces and edges.
- `RegionId` is the capture-local face ordinal (`source.rs:262-268`) and is
  stored in `Parent::Region`; DESIGN forbids persisting capture-local numbers.
- Components are bare `usize` positions (`normal_depth/mod.rs:31`,
  `ImageKey::Cap(usize)`); selection order is never normalised
  (`tkbo capture/selection.rs:102-120`).
- First-match lookups: `walk` (`selection.rs:91`) and `captured_span`
  (`wrapped_candidate/source.rs:286-293`) find pieces by curve ID, which is
  wrong once one curve has several pieces.

### R8. Versioning and process

- Proof behaviors 1–4 are all live as a raw `u32` with inconsistent gates
  (`>= 3`, `3..=4`, `== 4`, `2..=4`); about 12 gate sites; no deletion task.
  `CylindricalCurveOptions::default()` is behavior 1, which cap normal depth
  refuses.
- Work counts are part of the replay identity, so every speed-up needs a new
  behavior version.
- Feature documents read as a lab notebook (timings such as 3.605 s vs 3.655 s)
  rather than a normative design.
- Two capture paths: test-only `wrapped_profile_capture` and TKBO
  `capture_planar_regions`. `tkv3d capture_owned_font_text` is public with no
  consumer.
- Proof math duplicated downstream: `collar_cover/interval.rs` (own interval
  type), `correlated.rs:87` (own Bézier derivative).

### Strengths to keep

Sound outward-rounded exact proofs; typed refusals; finite cancellable work;
exact affine cap pcurves (`native/faces.rs:248-267`); history with source
intervals, all parents and stable depth roles; isolation and detached-export
intent; the TKBO planar capture and material selection.

## Proposed design

### Rational on-cylinder representation

Pick a center angle `c` per piece group and fit a polynomial
`τ̃(u) ≈ tan((θ(u) − c)/2)` on each piece by Hermite interpolation, so endpoint
values and tangents are exact and joins are G1:

```text
C̃_r(u) = O + r·Rot_c[((1 − τ̃²)E0 + 2τ̃·E1) / (1 + τ̃²)] + z(u)·Z,   w = 1 + τ̃²
```

- Edges lie on the cylinder exactly, up to binary64 pole storage.
- The side surface between `C̃_r0` and `C̃_r1`, with the same weights, has
  exactly radial rulings: an exact normal extrusion of the curve
  `(c + 2·atan τ̃, z)`.
- Caps stay analytic `GeomCylindricalSurface` with today's exact pcurves
  `(θ0 + s/R, z)`.
- The only error is a circumferential shift `R·Δ(u)`,
  `Δ = 2·atan τ̃ − (θ − c)`, a horizontal shift in the developed plane; `z` is
  exact. The cap-edge SameParameter deviation is `2r·sin(|Δ|/2)`.

Claim of a successful result: the exact normal extrusion of a profile within
`ε` of the authored profile, with the same topology.

Feasibility (checked in source):

- Rational constructors exist: `GeomBezierCurve::with_weights`
  (`tkg3d/src/geom/bezier_curve.rs:43`), `GeomBSplineCurve::with_weights`
  (`bspline_curve.rs:84,98`), `GeomBezierSurface::with_weights`
  (`bezier_surface.rs:83`), `GeomBSplineSurface::with_weights`
  (`bspline_surface.rs:164`), and 2D equivalents in `tkg2d`.
- BinTools reads and writes rational curves and surfaces
  (`tkbrep/src/bintools/curve_write.rs:111-123`, `curve_read.rs:90-96`,
  `surface_write.rs:169-222`, `surface_read.rs:167-192`,
  `curve2d_read.rs:87-105`).
- `GeomFillBezierCurves::with_two` accepts rational input
  (`tkgeomalgo/src/geom_fill/bezier_curves.rs:150-200`); a direct control net is
  equally simple.
- Maximum degree 25; a cubic source with cubic `τ̃` gives degree 9.

Gaps:

- The wrapped capture and validation stack accepts only cubic polynomial input
  (`capture/curves.rs:22,39`, `capture/surfaces.rs:41`,
  `native_validation/representations/curves.rs:40`, `rebind_all/vertex.rs:101`).
  It is replaced, not extended; the polynomial path is deleted.
- `certify_cylindrical_curve` refuses weights and degree above 3
  (`cylindrical_map/admission.rs:53-62`).
- Weight positivity: admit only when every Bernstein coefficient of `τ̃` has
  magnitude below 1. Near-equal weights may be stored as non-rational, which is
  harmless only when `τ̃ ≡ 0`.
- Shared vertices must come from one computation and be written as pole 0
  (`require_endpoints`, `native.rs:95-110`, needs bit-identical endpoints).
- Booleans and fillets on degree-9 rational ruled faces are untested (#420).
- New code: the `τ̃` Hermite fitter (`τ' = φ'(1 + τ²)/2`).

Fallback if rational edges are rejected: build the second-depth Hermite curve
as an exact xy scaling of the first about the axis (exact for Hermite in exact
arithmetic). Rulings become radial, but edges stay off the cylinder.

### Remaining obligations under one budget

| # | Obligation | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| O1 | 2D admission | Existing planar capture plus certified clearance `δ` between nonadjacent pieces, regularity margin `m` and join angles |
| O2 | Angular fit per piece | `\|Δ\| ≤ ε_θ` and a `\|Δ'\|` bound; depth-independent, certified once. Reuse `cylindrical_map/trig.rs`; bound `X − rW·cos θ` and `Y − rW·sin θ` from stored homogeneous coefficients (no atan) |
| O3 | Storage rounding | Exact, trig-free bounds on `\|X² + Y² − r²W²\|` and on ruling radiality between the `r0` and `r1` poles |
| O4 | Topology preserved | Straight 2D homotopy: `2R·ε_θ < δ` for nonadjacent pieces; `Δ = 0` at shared endpoints; `R·\|Δ'\| < m·sin(join half-angle)`. Reuse `cap_correspondence` with an envelope term |
| O5 | Native checks | Ordered topology, geometry (labelled sampled), self-intersection (labelled native search), tolerance; edge tolerances set to proven bounds |
| O6 | Measure | `V = A·(r1² − r0²)/(2R)`; `\|ΔA\| ≤ R·ε_θ·∫\|z'\|`; represented flux through `metric_bounds/rational_integrals.rs` |

Budget object: for a requested `ε`, assign `R·ε_θ ≤ ε/4` and rounding
`≤ ε/1000`, and require `δ ≥ 4ε`; otherwise refuse with the measured values.

Proof families removed: collar covers and warps, seam inverse and cap-side
seam, cap and side normal depth, 3D separating and join planes, side correction,
seam-gluing homotopy, and most of `rebind_all`.

### Algorithms

- A priori piece sizing: Hermite error is about `h⁴/384 · max|τ⁗|`; choose the
  dyadic level from it, never from prover exhaustion. Piece identity is then
  `(authored curve, canonical dyadic interval)`.
- Bernstein basis, de Casteljau subdivision, cached binomials and outward
  dyadic intervals instead of gcd-reduced rationals; round Taylor centers to
  `trig_bits`.
- 2D broad phase (sweep-and-prune or BVH) and adaptive-axis Bernstein hull
  separation reusing `bezier_pair/hull_separation.rs`: about N log N pairs.
- Point-witness refutation before subdivision.
- Proof cache keyed by exact coefficients; one angular certificate serves both
  depths.

### Patterns

- One orchestrator shaped like `surface_sketch/pipeline.rs`, with typed stages
  `Admitted → Constructed → Frozen → Validated → Measured → Published` and
  private constructors.
- Freeze once: encode, decode once, prove against the decoded bytes, publish
  the same bytes.
- Proof-obligation tree written before code, with one producer per obligation
  and a fixed share of `ε`.
- Identity: material by authored labels, never ordinals; indexed lookups that
  refuse ambiguity.
- One typed behavior enum with a deletion task per old version; work receipts
  are diagnostics, not replay identity.
