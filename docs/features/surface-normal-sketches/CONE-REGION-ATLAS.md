# Cone chart atlas for disconnected regions

`DirectedRegionAtlasRequireCertifiedV4` selects a separate rational fit
chart for each disconnected region. Each region uses its complete authored
boundary, including its holes. The fit center can change from one region
to the next. The developed coordinates, physical angular datum, and
normal-depth convention are shared across the request.

Use `normal_extrude_cone_profile_with_selection` and a directed planar
proof selector such as `GeometricCompositeV6`. The atlas uses the same
source capture, rational side construction, analytic caps, and
frozen-body publication sequence as the common chart.
Use `region_chart_domain_receipt` with an authored region ID to inspect
the executed chart. The domain policy entry also selects the directed
path when given `PositiveXRegionAtlasV5`.

## Periodic separation

The reference uses the shared displacement bound for every member. It
expands each authored physical angular interval by the angular effect of
that displacement at the member's certified distance from the apex. It shifts each interval by an integer multiple of the bounded
physical period and sorts the shifted intervals. It checks every adjacent
gap and the gap from the last interval back to the first through a full
period. The family can span more than one revolution in the authored lift
when these checks prove the intervals disjoint on the physical cone.

For the stored-solid proof, each analytic cap contributes a Cartesian
enclosure of its full UV rectangle. Each ruled side contributes the hull
of its actual two positive-weight control rows. A Cartesian box enclosing
the complete boundary encloses the resulting solid. The certificate
requires strictly disjoint member boxes and retains the existing
within-member side, cap, and incidence checks. An overlapping box can
leave separation unresolved even when the solids are disjoint.

The atlas fits one positive-X chart per connected region. A connected
region that needs several fit charts requires the connected atlas path.
The [placed cone entry](CONE-PLACEMENT.md) can place the completed atlas
in an oblique frame. Cone-face attachment remains separate work.

## Joins in decoded angle and slant

requires the same physical datum, cap carriers and slant origin across
the contour. Both decoded cap rows must agree. At each join, the
original Cartesian endpoints must agree exactly and the retained
unwrapped UV endpoints must have identical bits.

For an authored source `(x,y)`, let `q = x² + y²`. The angle rate is
`(x y' - y x') / (q sin α)` and the slant rate is `q' / (2 sqrt(q))`.

controls. It also encloses the derivative of the retained cap cubic

straight interpolation in angle and slant. This proves the local
adjacent-span condition. Nonadjacent and periodic separation during
the interpolation require their own checks.

## Signed volume of the decoded cap reference

The retained cap polynomials give the signed planar area
`A = ∮ v du` and first slant moment `B = ½ ∮ v² du` in angle `u`
and relative slant `v`. Bounded Bernstein polynomial integration
encloses both integrals. With semi-angle `α`, slant reference `L₀`,
depth midpoint `d̄` and outward sign `s`, the signed normal volume is
`Δd * ((L₀ sin α + s d̄ cos α) A + sin α B)`. The check refuses
unless the radius is proved positive at the minimum slant and signed
depth. An independent rectangle checks both normal senses and refusal
when inward extrusion crosses the axis.

## Filled normal-reference displacement

The cap join receipt now retains the maximum angular and slant
residuals separately. Denote them by `δu` and `δL`. The cap's
Bernstein slant hull, expanded by `δL`, encloses the entire
interpolation. The extrema of a linear slant coordinate over a
bounded fill lie on its boundary, so this hull also encloses the
interior. The meridian calculation bounds the radius of that hull
at every normal depth. It requires a strictly positive lower bound.

At a fixed depth, moving slant along the cone meridian costs at most
`δL`. The angular move at the destination radius costs at most
`Rmax * δu` by the chord inequality. Their sum bounds the distance
between any two points along one boundary parameter's interpolation.
If a chart point belongs to one endpoint fill and lies outside the
other, its winding can change only when the interpolated boundary
crosses it. The corresponding point on the destination boundary is
therefore within the same bound in world space. This argument applies
in both directions and at every depth. Periodic filled-overlap
admission remains a separate condition for the exact reference solid.

## Public connected atlas

`ConnectedChartAtlasV6` runs through the shared cone operation and
requires an explicit V5 or V6 planar proof. It takes the shared slant
datum from the authored region with the smallest semantic key. The
authored curves must lie in the certified positive-X developed wedge.
The operation uses the requested fit and integration limits. The
decoded cap profiles use one UV integral program across the family.
The operation checks periodic winding before measuring any reference
solid. An overlapping image reports both region keys. The cone result
retains the decoded cap receipts in request order.

The closest points of disjoint closed fills on the periodic cover
reach their boundaries. Over the full depth interval, slant and depth
are orthogonal in the meridian plane. The shorter angular chord is at
least half the shortest angular difference times the certified minimum
radius. The world-space lower bound uses the smaller of this angular
scale and one. The decoded caps and sides have pointwise correspondence
with the reference shell. Outside the storage-error neighborhood,
their straight interpolation preserves the shell winding, so removing
two storage-error bounds leaves a lower gap for the decoded solids.
The operation also requires ordered native validity for each member.
Native intersection search remains labeled `NativeSearch`.

enclosure width for both normal senses.
